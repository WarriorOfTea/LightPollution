# CIE colour data
> Дата создания: 2026-11-11

International Commission on Illumination (CIE), XYZ1931, LMS2006, illuminant D65.
Raw CSV/metadata are unchanged; DOI, official URLs, SHA256 and rights are in
`provenance.json`. All tables: **CC BY-SA4.0**
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Linear resampling and
zero extrapolation are performed at runtime; missing S above615nm lies
outside its declared range. No physical stellar flux is rescaled for colour.
